Independent advisory from Monaco

Storico Collectibles Advisory advises private clients, family offices and institutions on the acquisition, structuring and legal protection of rare assets. The firm sells nothing: no inventory, no collection, and no interest in the outcome other than its client's.

A largely unregulated market exposes buyers to risks that remain invisible: defective title, incomplete provenance, undervaluation, inadequate tax treatment. Every file therefore begins with the same review.

Our approach

Advisory beyond the transaction.

Four rules, held on every mandate. They are not selling points: they are professional obligations inherited from the practice of law.

01

Discretion

Every mandate is handled under strict confidentiality. No public listing, no intermediary without your express consent, no client named without written permission.

02

Precision

Legal rigour applied to the detail: from title verification to cross-border structuring, including the drafting of warranties and the escrow of the price.

03

Independence

No inventory, no collection, no seller's retrocession. Fees are agreed at engagement and do not depend on the deal closing: that is the condition for being able to advise walking away.

04

Network

Access to a selected network of specialists: experts, restorers, shipyards, auction houses and private sellers, in Europe and beyond.

How a file proceeds

Five stages, in this order. None begins before the previous one is closed.

I

Engagement

Written scope, fees agreed in advance, conflicts of interest cleared before any work begins.

II

Investigation

The file is rebuilt from source: registers, factory archives, deeds, invoices, published records. What the seller provides is only a starting point.

III

Independent valuation

Technical inspection by an independent specialist, three years of auction comparables, a discount applied to every weakness in the file.

IV

Negotiation

Conducted on the client's behalf, in their name or under the firm's cover where discretion requires it.

V

Structuring and deed

Choice of holding vehicle, tax arbitrage, drafting of warranties, escrow, transfer and safekeeping of the file.
