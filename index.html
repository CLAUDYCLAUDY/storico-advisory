Independent advisory from Monaco

Storico Collectibles Advisory supports private clients, family offices and institutions in acquiring, structuring and securing rare assets. Our team sells nothing: no inventory, no collection, no interest in the outcome other than our client's.

Collectibles are no longer a sentimental footnote in a portfolio: they are an asset class in their own right, weakly correlated with financial markets, yet without the regulatory protection surrounding them. A dedicated desk, engaged before you commit, is what separates an investment from a wager.

A largely unregulated market exposes buyers to risks that remain invisible: defective title, incomplete provenance, undervaluation, inadequate tax treatment. Every file therefore begins with the same review.

Seller side

Prepare the sale, |and be protected by it.

A sale is not over on the day the funds arrive. It is over on the day the buyer can no longer come back at you. Our team prepares the seller's file before the asset goes to market, serving both sides: yours, and that of a buyer who will pay all the better for seeing clearly.

01

The file

Independent technical inspection, dated and photographed condition report, reconstructed chain of provenance, service and restoration invoices gathered, identity checks recorded. Everything a buyer would demand, produced before they ask.

02

The disclosures

What you state binds you, and what you leave unsaid binds you further. Reserves, restorations, replacements and uncertainties are set out in writing, annexed to the contract and countersigned. Organised transparency is the seller's strongest protection.

03

After the sale

A disappointed buyer has remedies that long outlive the transaction: hidden defects, mistake as to an essential quality, breach of the duty to inform, fraudulent concealment. These claims can unwind the sale or reduce the price years later. A file assembled beforehand leaves them nothing to stand on.

A reassured buyer pays more, pays sooner, and does not come back.

Uncertainty has a price, and it is the seller who pays it, in the form of a discount, a delay, or a buyer pool narrowed to those who know how to exploit it. A complete file reverses that: it opens the asset to demanding buyers, the very ones who pay the top of the range, and it turns the negotiation into a simple verification.

Our approach

Advisory beyond the transaction.

Four rules, held on every mandate. Our team brings together French-qualified lawyers, sector experts and structuring specialists: these rules are those our organisation sets itself, and those its lawyers owe to their bar.

01

Discretion

Every mandate is handled under strict confidentiality. No public listing, no intermediary without your express consent, no client named without written permission.

02

Precision

The same rigour is applied to the detail: title verification, documentary review, drafting of warranties, escrow of the price, cross-border structuring. Points of law are handled by the admitted lawyers on the team and confirmed in each country by qualified local counsel.

03

Independence

No inventory, no collection, no seller's retrocession. Fees are agreed at engagement and do not depend on the deal closing: that is the condition for being able to advise walking away.

04

Network

A selected network of experts, restorers, shipyards, auction houses and local counsel across the market's principal centres: Monaco, Paris, Geneva, Zurich, Luxembourg, London, Milan, New York.

How a file proceeds

Five stages, in this order. None begins before the previous one is closed.

I

Engagement

Written scope, fees agreed in advance, conflicts of interest cleared before any work begins.

II

Investigation

The file is rebuilt from source: registers, factory archives, deeds, invoices, published records. What the seller provides is only a starting point.

III

Independent valuation

Technical inspection by an independent specialist, three years of auction comparables, a discount applied to every weakness in the file.

IV

Negotiation

Conducted on the client's behalf, in their name or under our team's cover where discretion requires it.

V

Structuring and deed

Choice of holding vehicle, tax arbitrage, drafting of warranties, escrow, transfer and safekeeping of the file.
